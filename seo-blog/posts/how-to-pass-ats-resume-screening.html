How to Pass ATS Resume Screening in 2026

The Problem: According to SHRM (Society for Human Resource Management), approximately 75% of job applications are rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before a human ever sees them.[1]

What Is an ATS?

An ATS is software used by employers to filter and rank job applications. These systems scan resumes for keywords, formatting, and relevant experience before passing qualified candidates to hiring managers.

Key Strategies to Pass ATS Screening

1. Use Standard Resume Formats

Avoid columns, tables, and graphics. ATS systems read left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Complex layouts can cause parsing errors.[2]

2. Include Relevant Keywords

Mirror the language from the job description. If they ask for "project management," use that exact phrase—not "managed projects."

3. Quantify Your Achievements

Use numbers and percentages: "Increased sales by 25%" is stronger than "Improved sales performance."

4. Use Standard Section Headings

Use "Work Experience," "Education," "Skills"—not creative alternatives that confuse the parser.

Common ATS Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using headers/footers for contact info (ATS often misses these)
  • Embedding text in images or graphics
  • Using uncommon fonts or special characters
  • Over-optimizing with keyword stuffing (modern ATS penalizes this)
