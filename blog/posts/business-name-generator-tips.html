How to Choose a Business Name in 2026

The Challenge: Your business name is the foundation of your brand identity. A good name is memorable, easy to spell, and available across all relevant platforms.[1]

Characteristics of a Great Business Name

  • Memorable: Easy to remember after hearing once
  • Pronounceable: People can say it without confusion
  • Relevant: Hints at what your business does or its values
  • Different: Stands out from competitors
  • Scalable: Won't limit future growth or expansion

Business Naming Process

1. Brainstorm Core Concepts

List words related to your business, industry, values, and target audience. Use metaphors, compound words, or invented words.

2. Check Availability

  • Domain name (use a domain checker)
  • Social media handles
  • Trademark database (USPTO.gov in the U.S.)
  • Business registration in your state

3. Test with Your Audience

Share your top choices with potential customers. Get feedback on memorability, pronunciation, and overall impression.

Naming Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using hard-to-spell words or unusual spellings
  • Picking a name too similar to competitors
  • Choosing a name that's too narrow (limits expansion)
  • Skipping trademark checks
  • Not checking domain availability
