How to Start a Business in 2026 — Step-by-Step Guide

The Opportunity: According to recent data, over 500,000 new businesses were registered in the U.S. alone in 2024, with small businesses accounting for 99.9% of all employers.[1] Starting a business has never been more accessible.

Step 1: Validate Your Business Idea

Before investing time and money, validate your idea through market research, surveys, and conversations with potential customers. Ask: Is there a real problem you're solving? Are people willing to pay for your solution?

Step 2: Choose a Business Name

Your name should be memorable, easy to spell, and available as a domain. Use tools like NameCraft's free business name generator to brainstorm options and check availability.

Step 3: Register Your Business

Register your business name with your state, obtain necessary licenses and permits, and get an Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS if you haven't already.

Step 4: Set Up Your Online Presence

  • Register your domain name
  • Create a professional website (use LandingCraft for free landing pages)
  • Set up social media profiles
  • Create business email addresses

Step 5: Create Your Brand Identity

Develop your brand's visual identity (logo, colors, typography) and voice (tone, messaging). Consistency across all platforms builds trust and recognition.

Step 6: Launch and Market

Start with a soft launch to gather feedback, then plan your official launch. Use social media, email marketing, and content marketing to drive awareness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Skipping market research
  • Underestimating startup costs
  • Trying to do everything alone
  • Neglecting customer feedback
  • Running out of cash before profitability
