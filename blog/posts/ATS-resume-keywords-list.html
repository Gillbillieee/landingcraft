Top ATS Resume Keywords for 2026 — Get Past the Bot

The Problem: According to SHRM, approximately 75% of resumes are rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before a human ever sees them.[1] Understanding ATS keywords is critical for getting your resume noticed.

What Are ATS Keywords?

ATS keywords are specific words and phrases that applicant tracking systems scan for when evaluating resumes. These typically match the requirements listed in job descriptions.

Technology

JavaScript, Python, React, Node.js, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Agile, Scrum, REST API, microservices, cloud computing, machine learning, data structures, algorithms

Marketing

Digital marketing, SEO, SEM, content marketing, social media marketing, email marketing, PPC, Google Analytics, conversion optimization, A/B testing, brand management, market research

Finance

Financial analysis, budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, GAAP, IFRS, Excel, financial modeling, risk management, internal controls, audit preparation

Healthcare

Patient care, electronic health records (EHR), HIPAA compliance, clinical trials, medical terminology, patient advocacy, healthcare administration, quality improvement

How to Use ATS Keywords Effectively

  1. Analyze job descriptions: Identify repeated keywords and phrases
  2. Incorporate naturally: Don't keyword stuff — use terms in context
  3. Mirror the language: If they say "project management," use that exact phrase
  4. Include both acronyms and full terms: e.g., "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)"

ATS Optimization Checklist

  • Use standard section headings (Work Experience, Education, Skills)
  • Avoid tables, columns, and graphics
  • Use common fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)
  • Save as .docx or PDF (check job posting for preference)
  • Include a skills section with relevant keywords
