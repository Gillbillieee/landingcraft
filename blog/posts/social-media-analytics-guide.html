Social Media Analytics Guide for 2026

The Data-Driven Era: According to Hootsuite's 2025 Social Media Trends Report, 73% of businesses now use social media analytics to guide their marketing decisions.[1]

Key Social Media Metrics to Track

Engagement Metrics

  • Likes, comments, shares, saves
  • Engagement rate (engagement ÷ followers × 100)
  • Average engagement per post

Reach Metrics

  • Impressions (total times content is seen)
  • Reach (unique users who saw your content)
  • Follower growth rate

Conversion Metrics

  • Click-through rate (CTR) to your website
  • Conversion rate from social traffic
  • Cost per acquisition (CPA)

Analytics Tools for Small Businesses

  • Native platform analytics: Facebook Insights, Instagram Insights, Twitter Analytics
  • Third-party tools: Hootsuite, Buffer, Sprout Social
  • Web analytics: Google Analytics for tracking social traffic

Creating an Analytics Report

  1. Define your goals (awareness, engagement, conversions)
  2. Choose relevant metrics for each goal
  3. Set benchmarks and targets
  4. Track performance monthly
  5. Analyze trends and adjust strategy

Common Analytics Mistakes

  • Focusing on vanity metrics (followers) instead of meaningful ones
  • Not setting clear goals before tracking
  • Ignoring negative data or underperforming content
  • Failing to compare against industry benchmarks
