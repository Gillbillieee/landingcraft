Local SEO Guide for Small Business in 2026

The Opportunity: 46% of all Google searches have local intent, and 78% of local mobile searches result in an offline purchase within 24 hours.[1]

Google My Business Optimization

  • Claim and verify your Google Business Profile
  • Complete every section of your profile
  • Add high-quality photos of your business, products, and team
  • Post regular updates about events, offers, and news
  • Respond to all reviews (positive and negative)

Local Citation Building

Citations are mentions of your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) across the web. Consistency is critical — ensure your NAP is identical across all platforms.

Key Citation Sources

  • Google Business Profile
  • Yelp
  • Bing Places
  • Apple Maps
  • Industry-specific directories
  • Local chamber of commerce websites

Review Management Strategy

  • Ask satisfied customers to leave reviews
  • Respond to all reviews promptly and professionally
  • Address negative reviews constructively
  • Aim for consistent 4+ star ratings

Local SEO Checklist

  1. Optimize website for local keywords
  2. Create location-specific landing pages
  3. Build local backlinks through partnerships
  4. List your business in local directories
  5. Generate consistent reviews
