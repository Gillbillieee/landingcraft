Remote Work Resume Tips for 2026 — Land Your Dream Remote Job

The Opportunity: According to FlexJobs, remote job postings increased by 244% between 2019 and 2025, with over 30% of U.S. jobs now offering some form of remote work.[1]

What Employers Look for in Remote Workers

  • Self-motivation: Ability to work independently without supervision
  • Communication skills: Clear written and verbal communication
  • Time management: Meeting deadlines and managing multiple projects
  • Tech proficiency: Comfort with collaboration tools (Slack, Zoom, Notion)
  • Reliability: Consistent performance and responsiveness

Resume Sections for Remote Job Applications

Professional Summary

Highlight remote work experience, relevant skills, and what makes you a strong remote worker.

Remote Work Experience

  • List any previous remote or hybrid work experience
  • Mention tools and platforms used for collaboration
  • Include metrics showing productivity in remote settings

Technical Skills

List relevant software, communication tools, and technical competencies. Be specific about proficiency levels.

Remote Job Resume Mistakes to Avoid

  • Failing to highlight remote-specific skills
  • Not mentioning time zones or availability
  • Ignoring the importance of written communication samples
  • Using a generic resume for every application
