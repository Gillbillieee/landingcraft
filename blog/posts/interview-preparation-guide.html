Interview Preparation Guide for 2026 — Ace Every Interview

The Reality: According to CareerBuilder, 70% of employers use behavioral questions in interviews.[1] Being prepared with specific examples using the STAR method can significantly improve your chances.

Before the Interview

Research the Company

  • Understand their mission, values, and products/services
  • Review recent news, press releases, and social media
  • Know your interviewers (LinkedIn profiles)
  • Understand the role's requirements and how they match your skills

Prepare Your Stories

Common Interview Questions

  1. "Tell me about yourself"
  2. "Why do you want to work here?"
  3. "What are your strengths and weaknesses?"
  4. "Describe a challenge you overcame"
  5. "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"

Interview Day Tips

  • Arrive 10-15 minutes early (or log in 5 minutes early for virtual)
  • Dress appropriately for the company culture
  • Bring extra copies of your resume
  • Prepare thoughtful questions to ask the interviewer
  • Practice good body language (eye contact, firm handshake)

After the Interview

Send a thank-you email within 24 hours. Reference specific topics discussed and reiterate your interest in the position.
